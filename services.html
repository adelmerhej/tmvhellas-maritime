Services

One partner for the whole voyage.

Six services that cover every stage of a maritime venture. Use one on its own, or let us combine them into a single, accountable programme.

01

Project Development

We manage all phases of maritime and trade projects, from initial concept through execution. Drawing on the group's track record of founding and operating shipping companies across four countries, we structure the project, assemble the partners and keep every phase on schedule.

Discuss this service
  • Feasibility studies and project structuring
  • Partner, financing and counterparty sourcing
  • Technical specification and vessel selection
  • Execution oversight through to handover
02

Ship Sale & Purchase

Our team handles ship acquisitions and sales from identifying the right vessel through to commissioning, so owners and investors can move decisively when the market offers an opportunity.

Discuss this service
  • Market screening and vessel identification
  • Pre-purchase inspection coordination
  • Negotiation and contract support (MOA)
  • Delivery, flagging and commissioning
03

Technical & Crew Management

In collaboration with Columbia Ship Management, we provide technical and crew management designed to optimise ship performance, reduce breakdown and off-hire time and keep every vessel employed and earning.

Discuss this service
  • Planned maintenance and dry-docking supervision
  • Crew selection, training and welfare
  • Safety, quality and regulatory compliance
  • Performance monitoring to minimise off-hire
04

Bunkering & Fuel Services

Through our partnership with Baseblue, one of the world's largest suppliers of bunker fuel and lubricating oil, we secure competitive fuel and lube prices and can hedge long-term contracts for our clients.

Discuss this service
  • Fuel oil and lubricant supply worldwide
  • Competitive pricing through Baseblue's scale
  • Hedging options for long-term contracts
  • Quality and quantity assurance at delivery
05

Commercial Services

Our commercial services cover chartering, voyage optimisation and cargo handling, applying the group's own operating experience in bulk carriers and product tankers to each fixture.

Discuss this service
  • Time and voyage chartering
  • Voyage optimisation and routing
  • Cargo handling and port coordination
  • Post-fixture operations and claims support
06

Expert Consultancy

We advise on tanker-sector investments, operations and market analysis, offering clients the perspective of a family that has owned, managed and traded ships for more than a century.

Discuss this service
  • Tanker-sector investment appraisal
  • Operational reviews and cost benchmarking
  • Market analysis and freight outlooks
  • Strategic advice on fleet renewal and trade projects
