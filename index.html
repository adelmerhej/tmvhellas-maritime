Who we are

A premier maritime services provider, built on four generations of shipping.

Titan Maritime Ventures (Hellas) Ltd offers tailored solutions for global shipping and trade. Based in Athens and part of the Titan Commercial & Investment Group, we specialise in project development, ship management and commercial operations for the tanker and dry bulk trades.

  • One-stop shop

    Every stage of a maritime venture under one roof, from concept and acquisition to operation and disposal.

  • Global partnerships

    Technical and crew management with Columbia Ship Management, fuel and lubricants with Baseblue.

  • Asset-light model

    Economies of scale from our partners, without the overhead of a full in-house operation.
