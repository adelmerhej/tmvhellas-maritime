Company

A family shipping story, written since 1902.

Titan Maritime Ventures (Hellas) Ltd is the Greek arm of a maritime group whose roots run back to the Port of Beirut. Today it combines that heritage with world-class partners to deliver one-stop maritime solutions.

4 citiesBeirut · Dubai · Bucharest · Athens
Who we are

Premier maritime services, tailored to each venture.

Based in Athens and backed by over a century of maritime experience through the Titan Commercial & Investment Group, we specialise in project development, ship management and commercial operations. Our services span every stage of a maritime venture, from initial concept to daily operations.

Strategic partnerships with Columbia Ship Management and Baseblue let us offer technical and crew management, fuel and lubricant supply at a scale that few independent operators can match, while keeping a single accountable team in front of the client.

History

From the Port of Beirut to the Saronic Gulf.

Four milestones that shaped the group behind Titan Maritime Ventures.

1902

Beirut: the only licensed lighterage company

The Jamil family founds a Lebanese shipping company in Beirut, the port's only licensed lighterage company, and still is. It grows into the largest stevedoring operator at the Port of Beirut and expands into ship agency, terminal operations, shipbroking and chartering.

2003

Dubai: Qawareb Ship Management

The family incorporates Qawareb Ship Management LLC in Dubai, owning and operating a fleet of twelve handysize and multipurpose vessels.

2016

Bucharest: Titan Commercial & Investment

A fully owned European entity is founded for maritime and trade project development and shipping consultancy. It operates Panamax, Ultramax and Supramax bulk carriers carrying grain and iron ore, and MR, LR1 and LR2 tankers, and holds minority shares in two MRs and two Supramaxes.

2021

Athens: Titan Maritime Ventures (Hellas)

Incorporated in Greece to deliver technical and crew management in collaboration with Columbia Ship Management, and fuel bunkering services with Baseblue, alongside project development, sale and purchase, commercial services and consultancy.
